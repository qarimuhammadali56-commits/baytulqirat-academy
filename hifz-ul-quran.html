QURAN MEMORIZATION COURSE

Hifz-ul-Quran Course

Memorize the Holy Quran with a structured learning plan, daily guidance, regular revision and one-to-one support from a qualified Quran teacher.

What You Will Learn

Students memorize the Holy Quran step by step with correct pronunciation, Tajweed and regular revision.

Course Objective

Build strong memorization habits and help students retain their lessons through continuous revision and daily practice.

Who Can Join?

Suitable for children, teenagers and adults who want to memorize the Holy Quran with proper guidance.

Online Classes

One-to-one online Hifz classes through Zoom or WhatsApp video call with flexible timings.

Why Learn Hifz-ul-Quran?

Hifz-ul-Quran is a beautiful journey of memorizing and preserving the words of Allah. With regular lessons, revision and personal guidance, students can develop a consistent routine and improve the quality of their Quran memorization.
