QURAN LEARNING COURSE

Noorani Qaida Course

Learn the fundamentals of Quran reading from the beginning with correct pronunciation, Makharij and basic Tajweed rules.

What You Will Learn

Arabic letters, correct pronunciation, joining letters, Harakat, Sukoon, Madd and essential Quran reading rules.

Course Objective

Build a strong foundation so students can read the Holy Quran correctly and confidently.

Who Can Join?

Suitable for children, beginners, adults and students who want to improve their Quran reading.

Online Classes

One-to-one online Quran classes through Zoom or WhatsApp video call with flexible timings.

Why Learn Noorani Qaida?

Noorani Qaida is an important foundation for learning to read the Holy Quran. Students learn Arabic letters, correct pronunciation and basic reading rules step by step before moving towards fluent Quran reading.
