QURAN RECITATION & MAQAMAT

Maqamat Course

Learn the beautiful Maqamat used in Quran recitation and improve your voice, tone and recitation style with professional guidance.

What You Will Learn

Introduction to Maqamat, voice control, tone, transitions and practical Quran recitation.

Course Objective

Help students understand and practice beautiful recitation styles while maintaining correct Tajweed.

Who Can Join?

Quran students, reciters and anyone interested in improving their recitation style.

Online Classes

Personal online guidance through Zoom or WhatsApp video call.

7 Maqamat of Quran Recitation

🎵 Maqam Bayati
🎵 Maqam Hijaz
🎵 Maqam Saba
🎵 Maqam Rast
🎵 Maqam Nahawand
🎵 Maqam Kurd
🎵 Maqam Ajam

Why Learn Maqamat?

Maqamat can help a reciter develop better control of voice, tone and recitation style while continuing to observe the rules of Tajweed.
